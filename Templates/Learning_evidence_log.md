## Candidate capabilities grounded in the existing project
These are opportunities, not verified skill gains or additional assignments.

| Capability | Existing opportunity | Evidence the student can retain |
|---|---|---|
| Research planning and facilitation | Interviews, contextmapping and comprehension study | Approved preparation, supervisor feedback and student-authored reflection |
| Evidence-based design judgement | Design brief and concept selection | Student-authored decision rationale tied to the brief |
| Parametric modelling and controlled reuse | Production system and both prototype iterations | Versioned template, change record, measured production time and independent reuse |
| Service facilitation and handover | Choice Session and service blueprint | Student-created session material and appropriate review references |
| Scoping and commercial estimation | Sponsor review and actual paid work | Estimated versus actual hours, agreed scope and reasons for variance |
| Clear professional communication | Supervision, presentation and project webpage | Before/after presentation examples and audience feedback |

Prefer an example used successfully again over a course-completion badge. A skill should be described as developing until its evidence supports a stronger claim. Illustration/presentation assistance must be declared in the AI-use register and stay within the proposal's boundary.
