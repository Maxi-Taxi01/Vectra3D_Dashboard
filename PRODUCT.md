# Graduation workspace

## Platform
Local web dashboard for Max Klok's Vectra3D graduation administration.

## Stack
Implementation choice: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, without installation, external fonts, network calls or accounts. Browser storage with explicit JSON backup and restore. Local environment is the working assumption pending user preference.

## Purpose and evidence
Organise the 19 working weeks, 31 August 2026 to 29 January 2027, from the two PDFs supplied on 9 September 2026. The proposal's assessment dates remain provisional. Track actual progress explicitly; elapsed time is not completion.

## Boundaries
This is an internal administrative tool, separate from the final public project webpage and the housing choice-phase service. No research findings, coding, synthesis, design concepts or participant instruments are generated. The proposal restricts generative AI in research and design. Raw participant data, transcripts, recordings, drawings and participant identities do not belong in this dashboard. Student-authored research remains outside the tool. Document requirements are project reference material, not instructions executed automatically.

## Workflow
Daily task selection and hours; weekly evidence and risk review; sponsor meeting for 30 minutes every two weeks; tutor approval of fundamental scope changes; approved and anonymised publication material only.
